1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & ROLE HIERARCHY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The Provash Agency ecosystem operates on a 4-tier Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) 
architecture designed to ensure clean separation of concerns, high security, 
efficient client sprint delivery, and zero unauthorized data access:

   [LEVEL 1: SUPER ADMIN]  ──> Supreme Authority, Owner, Full Financial & System Control
          │
   [LEVEL 2: ADMIN]        ──> Agency Operations Head, Service/Portfolio CRUD, Staff Management
          │
   [LEVEL 3: PROJECT_MANAGER]──> Sprint Execution, Client Delivery, Revisions, Software Deployment
          │
   [LEVEL 4: USER / CLIENT] ──> End-Consumer, Service/SaaS Buyer, Consultation Applicant


================================================================================
2. COMPREHENSIVE ROLE PROFILES & RESPONSIBILITIES
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 1: SUPER_ADMIN (Platform Owner / Founder — Provash Sharma)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Who They Are:
   - The platform founder, lead engineer, and legal business owner.
   - Initialized via database seeds; cannot self-register from public endpoints.
   - Only a Super Admin can create another Super Admin.

B. Core Responsibilities & Powers:
   1. User & Admin Management:
      - Create, invite, promote, block, unblock, and permanently delete Admins.
      - Create, block, unblock, or manage other Super Admins.
      - Full access to audit trails, login session logs, and security anomaly logs.
   2. Financial & Payment Control:
      - Complete access to all payment gateway configurations (bKash, Nagad, Stripe, 
        SSLCommerz, Bank Transfers).
      - View gross revenue, net margins, platform transaction ledgers, and execute refunds.
      - Authority to adjust payment thresholds and corporate discount vouchers.
   3. Proprietary Software Suite & SaaS Architecture:
      - Absolute control over the 6 SaaS products (DevFlow ERP, TubePulse AI, 
        SocialBoost Hub, RestoPos Cloud, ShopNest Commerce, MedCare Plus).
      - Authority to update production Vercel demo URLs, GitHub repositories, and API webhooks.
   4. Infrastructure & Server Environment:
      - Access to environment variables (.env), database migration commands, and cloud storage.

C. Restrictions:
   - None within the system boundary. Cannot be blocked or deleted by any Admin, 
     Project Manager, or regular User.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 2: ADMIN (Agency Operations Head / Studio Manager)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Who They Are:
   - The operational head of the digital agency, hired or appointed by the Super Admin.
   - Created through admin invitation/onboarding; cannot self-register from the public site.

B. Core Responsibilities & Powers:
   1. Inquiries & Lead Processing:
      - Review and triage incoming client consultation requests from homepage forms.
      - Update inquiry lifecycle: PENDING -> CONTACTED -> IN_PROGRESS -> RESOLVED / CLOSED.
      - Add internal administrative notes to client lead profiles.
   2. Service & Portfolio Catalogue CRUD:
      - Publish, edit, reorder, or archive agency services (Web Dev, YouTube Automation, SMM).
      - Add new case studies and verified projects to the Portfolio Showcase.
      - Approve and publish client ratings, video reviews, and testimonials.
   3. Team & User Operations:
      - Onboard and manage Project Managers (assign roles, monitor active sprints).
      - View client directory, monitor client account statuses, and block abusive users.
      - Create other Admins when delegated by Super Admin.
   4. Client Invoicing:
      - Generate official PDF project invoices, milestone billing links, and verify bank receipts.

C. Restrictions & Boundaries:
   - CANNOT create, block, edit, or delete any Super Admin.
   - CANNOT modify core database schemas, secret API keys, or root environment configurations.
   - CANNOT execute financial account withdrawals or override core gateway accounts without Super Admin approval.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 3: PROJECT_MANAGER (Sprint Executor / Technical Coordinator / Client Lead)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Who They Are:
   - The direct bridge between agency leadership and the client.
   - Leads project sprints, oversees code/content deliverables, and manages client satisfaction.
   - Created and assigned by Super Admin or Admin; cannot self-register.

B. Core Responsibilities & Powers:
   1. Client Sprint & Milestone Tracking:
      - Oversee the day-to-day development of client orders (e.g., Next.js web application, 
        YouTube channel automation setup, SMM campaigns).
      - Update active milestone progress on the Client Portal (e.g., "Architecture Setup", 
        "UI Implementation", "API Integration", "Staging QA", "Ready for Launch").
   2. Client Communication & Revision Management:
      - Directly interact with the client via the in-app messaging system or ticket board.
      - Collect client feedback, document scope adjustments, and ensure on-time milestone delivery.
      - Manage revision cycles according to the client's purchased plan (Starter, Growth, Enterprise).
   3. Software Setup & Production Deployment:
      - Assist clients who purchase turnkey SaaS products (e.g., deploying DevFlow ERP or 
        ShopNest Commerce from Vercel staging to the client's custom domain).
      - Deliver onboarding walkthroughs, documentation, and handover credentials to the client.
   4. Technical QA & Lead Verification:
      - Perform initial scope feasibility audits on complex inbound technical inquiries.
      - Review client assets (branding files, API credentials, domain access) before sprint launch.

C. Restrictions & Boundaries:
   - CANNOT change service catalogue prices, tier packages, or company-wide rates.
   - CANNOT view aggregate agency revenues, financial balances, or other PMs' private client accounts.
   - CANNOT delete users, create admins, or alter platform-wide security settings.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ROLE 4: USER / CLIENT (Customer, Startup Founder, Creator, Business Client)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A. Who They Are:
   - The end-consumer, client, or startup seeking web engineering, YouTube automation, 
     social media marketing, or turnkey software licenses.
   - Can freely self-register via Email/Password or one-click Google OAuth.

B. Core Responsibilities & Powers:
   1. Service Discovery & Interactive Demos:
      - Browse full agency solutions: Modular Frontend, Modular Backend, and Turnkey Fullstack.
      - Test live interactive software demos on Vercel across all 6 proprietary SaaS products.
   2. Quotes & Consultation Applications:
      - Submit customized project proposals with budget ranges, tech preferences, and launch targets.
   3. Instant Checkout & Online Payments:
      - Purchase agency plans (Starter Launchpad, Growth Business, Enterprise Elite) or software.
      - Pay securely via bKash, Nagad, Visa, Mastercard, Amex, or International Stripe.
   4. Authenticated Client Portal Experience:
      - Real-time sprint progress dashboard showing current milestone, completion percentage, and ETA.
      - Download official tax invoices, digital payment receipts, and contract deliverables.
      - Submit revision tickets and communicate directly with their assigned Project Manager.

C. Restrictions & Boundaries:
   - Zero access to internal admin endpoints, studio management screens, or other clients' projects.
   - Can only read and update their own personal profile, purchased services, and private invoices.

4. END-TO-END COLLABORATION WORKFLOW SCENARIO
================================================================================
Example: Client orders a Custom Web Application or DevFlow ERP software.

Step 1: USER / CLIENT
   - Browses the website, explores the 6 software demos on Vercel, and selects an Enterprise Plan.
   - Fills out the consultation form or completes instant payment via bKash/Stripe.
   - Receives an automated booking confirmation and access to their Client Portal.

Step 2: ADMIN
   - Receives notification of new order/inquiry in the Admin Management Panel.
   - Verifies payment status and scopes out requirements.
   - Assigns a dedicated PROJECT_MANAGER to lead the sprint and links them to the project.

Step 3: PROJECT_MANAGER
   - Welcomes the client via the Client Portal board and gathers specific brand guidelines/APIs.
   - Configures the development milestones (e.g., Milestone 1: Wireframe, Milestone 2: Backend).
   - Updates milestone progress in real-time, conducts sprint demos, and tests deployment on Vercel.
   - Handles client revision requests and delivers the final production app to the client's domain.

Step 4: SUPER_ADMIN
   - Monitors overall agency performance, on-time delivery benchmarks, and total revenues.
   - Oversees high-level quality standards and steps in for strategic enterprise contracts.

5. DATA MODELS AND ENUMS INTEGRATION (FOR PRISMA & JWT AUTH)
================================================================================

Enum Definition:
enum Role {
  SUPER_ADMIN
  ADMIN
  PROJECT_MANAGER
  USER
}

Token Payload (JWT Access Token):
{
  "userId": "uuid-v4",
  "email": "user@example.com",
  "role": "PROJECT_MANAGER",
  "iat": 1700000000,
  "exp": 1700086400
}

Middleware Guard Route Standard (checkAuth utility):
- Public Routes: checkAuth not required.
- User Protected Routes: checkAuth("USER", "PROJECT_MANAGER", "ADMIN", "SUPER_ADMIN")
- Project Manager Routes: checkAuth("PROJECT_MANAGER", "ADMIN", "SUPER_ADMIN")
- Admin Management Routes: checkAuth("ADMIN", "SUPER_ADMIN")
- Super Admin Only Routes: checkAuth("SUPER_ADMIN")
